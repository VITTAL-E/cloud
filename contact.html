Principal-to-Principal FAQ

Direct answers to critical questions typically raised by Chief Technology Officers, VPs of Infrastructure, and Compliance Directors.

How do you guarantee zero downtime during database cutovers?

database

We use continuous Change Data Capture (CDC via Debezium or AWS DMS) combined with a dual-write replication bridge. Target clusters run in shadow validation mode under real synthetic workload replays before DNS and proxy pointers shift seamlessly in milliseconds.

Who owns the Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and automation scripts?

code

100% Client IP Ownership. All Terraform modules, Helm values, ArgoCD manifests, and CI/CD pipelines are authored directly in your Git repositories under your organization’s license. There is absolute zero vendor lock-in.

Do you support regulated environments (HIPAA, PCI-DSS, FedRAMP)?

security

Yes. We deploy using pre-hardened NIST 800-53 and CIS Benchmark blueprints. All transit and at-rest cryptographic key envelopes (KMS/Vault), immutable audit trails, and strict IAM boundaries are verified prior to production cutover.

What does your post-migration hypercare period look like?

clinical_notes

Every engagement includes 90 calendar days of 24/7 hypercare. Our SRE Fellows join your incident escalation rotations, run live Chaos Engineering drills with your team, and complete hands-on knowledge-transfer workshops for platform engineers.

How quickly can an engineering pod deploy into our repositories?

bolt

Following mutual NDA and access clearance, our pod commences Sprint 0 within 7 to 10 business days. We provide complete background check certifications and device posture verification before touching any production or staging environment.

Can you integrate directly with our internal platform engineers?

group_work

We operate almost exclusively as an embedded co-engineering force. We participate in your standups, author PRs for review by your team, and pair-program on complex ingress routing or stateful database operators.
